Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data 2ACE collects when you use our website and service, why, who receives it, and what rights you have. Questions: hello@2ace.pl.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is 2ACE spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością ("2ACE"), ul. Ostrobramska 101A lok. 301, 04-041 Warszawa, Poland, NIP 1133212948, REGON 545746743, registered in the National Court Register (KRS) under number 0001267111. Contact for anything about your data: hello@2ace.pl, telephone +48 608 180 946.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone, company name, tax ID, address, country and region.
  • Agreements: the name you typed to sign, the time, and your IP address, as proof of acceptance.
  • Your service data: the products, deliveries and stock you enter or send us, and photos we take of damaged goods at receiving.
  • Messages: what you write in the contact form or in requests, and our replies.
  • Payment data: card payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number. We keep your invoices and subscription status.
  • Domain registration: if we register a .pl domain for you, the company and contact details needed for the registration.
  • Technical data: your browser keeps a login session and a draft of your plan on your device. Our servers log requests for security.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service and manage your account, plan, deliveries and payments: performance of our contract with you.
  • To issue invoices and keep accounting records: a legal obligation.
  • To answer your questions, keep the service secure, prevent fraud and handle disputes: our legitimate interests.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage (our project is hosted in London, United Kingdom).
  • Stripe: payments and invoices.
  • Resend: sending our emails to you.
  • Hostinger: hosting of our website, and registration of .pl domains for customers. Domain registrant details are also held by the .pl registry (NASK).
  • Google Fonts: loads our fonts, so your browser contacts Google when you open a page.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, it is under an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

Who can see your account inside 2ACE

Our staff can open your account data to give you support and to handle your goods. Opening a customer's account is restricted to authorised staff, requires a stated reason, and is recorded in an audit log.

Cookies and similar storage

We use only the browser storage needed to keep you signed in and to remember the plan you are building. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Account and service data: while your account is active, then only as long as needed for legal claims.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 5 years from the end of the tax year, as Polish law requires.
  • Messages and receiving photos: as long as needed to resolve the matter they relate to.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable form. Write to hello@2ace.pl and we reply within one month. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), uodo.gov.pl.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we tell you by email or in your dashboard.
