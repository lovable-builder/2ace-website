Terms of service

This page sets out the terms you accept when you sign the 2ACE service agreement at checkout. If we have agreed a separate written contract with you, that contract applies where it differs.

The service

2ACE provides warehousing and fulfillment from Poland: dedicated storage space, receiving of your goods, order picking and shipping, returns handling, import and customs help, and optional extras such as a .pl storefront and 2ACE Market. You choose what is in your plan when you build it, and you can change it later from your dashboard.

Plans and term

Plans run monthly and roll over. There is no minimum term. You can cancel or resize with 30 days notice.

Prices and billing

  • Prices are in Polish zloty and exclude VAT. VAT is added where it applies.
  • Space is billed on the reserved square metres, in advance, on the 1st of the month. Add-ons are billed from the day they start.
  • Payment is by card through Stripe. You receive a VAT invoice for each payment, available in your account.
  • When you change your plan, we show you the difference before you confirm. Increases and decreases are prorated for the rest of the billing period.

Your space

Each shelf bin or pallet slot is assigned to one customer. Your goods are stored only in space assigned to you, and every movement of your stock is recorded. You can see your stock and deliveries in your dashboard.

Service levels

  • Orders received before 15:00 ship the same day.
  • Returned goods are inspected and graded within 48 hours.
  • Stock accuracy of 99.5%.

When a delivery arrives different from what you booked (fewer or more units, damaged or unexpected goods), we record it, photograph damaged goods, and tell you by email.

Insurance

Goods on our floor are insured at their declared purchase value.

Customs

For imports into Poland, we act as your indirect customs representative.

Domains

If your plan includes a .pl storefront, or you request a .pl domain from your dashboard, we register the domain for you through Hostinger, in your company's name. By signing, or by requesting the domain, you accept Hostinger's Domain Name Registration Agreement. A domain requested separately costs 99 zł one time (excluding VAT), first year included. Keep your registration details up to date; tell us about any change and we pass it on.

2ACE Market

If you sell through 2ACE Market, there is no setup or monthly fee. You pay a commission by product category, only when something sells. The current rates are shown when you build your plan.

Your data

How we handle personal data is described in our privacy policy.

Law and disputes

Polish law applies. Disputes are settled in the courts of Warsaw.

Contact

Questions about these terms: hello@2ace.pl.
