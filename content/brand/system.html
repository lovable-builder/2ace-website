In one minute

2ACE is a warehouse in Poland, and the software that runs it.

A brand rents space by the square metre, sends its goods in, and 2ACE receives them, stores them, picks and packs the orders, ships them and handles returns. The brand watches all of it, live, from one dashboard. The price is shown online before anyone talks to a salesperson.

Who it is for

Brands that sell in Europe but do not want to build a warehouse.

  • Overseas sellers, especially from China, whose goods arrive by sea, air or road and need customs cleared, storage and fast delivery to European buyers.
  • Small and growing European brands that have outgrown a spare room and want their own space, counted and insured, without a long contract.
  • New sellers who want a Polish web store (.pl) wired to their stock from the first day.

The problems they have

  • Goods disappear between the factory and the shelf: nobody can say where they are.
  • Costs are unclear until the invoice arrives, and every quote needs a call.
  • EU rules keep changing (customs duty on small parcels, packaging rules, product labelling).
  • Stock numbers are wrong, so orders are oversold or late.

How goods flow

The journey of a delivery, and of an order.

Goods coming in

1
The customer books the delivery in the dashboard: carrier, tracking, expected date, products and quantities.
2
The truck arrives. The team scans each product barcode on a phone, counts, and photographs anything damaged. If anything differs from the booking, the customer is emailed at once.
3
Good goods go straight into the customer's own bin and are available the same moment. No set-up, the bin is created automatically.
4
The customer sees the stock update live in the dashboard: available, reserved, incoming, set aside.

An order going out

1
The order arrives, typed in or imported from a CSV file. The delivery details are checked on entry (full name, phone, house number, postal code), so the shipping label cannot fail later.
2
Stock is reserved for the whole order at once. If something is short, the order is held with the reason and reserves itself when stock arrives.
3
Picking. The picker's phone shows the bin, the product with its photo and the quantity. One tap on "Picked".
4
Packing. Items are scanned into the box, the parcel is weighed and measured.
5
The label. The system compares carriers and buys the cheapest that fits, or staff choose. A customer can also bring their own label.
6
Shipped. The goods leave the stock records. The customer follows the order on a simple "journey" strip, from received to shipped.

For the screen: the journey strip is the most "filmable" idea in the product. The dashboard shows seven stations with a live count on each, and they light up as goods move: Booked, Arriving, On the shelf, Ordered, Picking, Packed, Shipped. Each order also has its own small tracker: Received, Reserved, Picking, Packed, Shipped.

What the team uses: the floor system.

The scan app (a phone page)

Four big buttons: Receive, Put away, Pick, Pack. Works with the phone camera or a hardware scanner. Built for gloves and speed.

The admin panel

Customers, orders, deliveries, stock, products, locations, approvals, requests, domains, shipping, bookings and an audit log. It works on a phone too.

  • Every movement is written down. Receiving, moving, picking, shipping: each is a permanent line in a ledger that cannot be edited afterwards. Mistakes are corrected with a new line, never by rewriting history.
  • Every staff action is logged in an audit trail: who did what, when, and why.
  • Roles. Admin, support and warehouse each see only what they need. Admin and support sign in with a second step (an authenticator code).

Shipping

Labels in seconds, from the carrier that fits.

2ACE connects to a shipping-label service that offers the main carriers: InPost, DPD, DHL, GLS and others such as UPS, FedEx, Poczta Polska and Orlen. For each packed order the system shows every carrier's price side by side, each with its logo badge, and can buy the cheapest one that takes the parcel.

  • Automatic mode: right after packing, the cheapest suitable carrier is bought. If one refuses, the next is tried. A person is told when something needs attention.
  • Safety limits: a switch to turn buying off instantly, a limit per label, a daily limit and a balance check. A label can never be bought twice for the same order.
  • Customer's own label: a seller who already has a label (from a marketplace or their carrier contract) uploads it and 2ACE prints and applies it.
  • If the details are wrong: staff can correct an order's delivery details, or email the customer asking for the correction, in one click.
