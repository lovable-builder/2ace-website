About 100 words

2ACE is a warehousing and fulfilment company in Warsaw, Poland. It is run by two partners, one Chinese and one Egyptian, who bring a long history in manufacturing, sales and operations and who live and work in China and in Poland. Brands that sell in Europe rent their own space by the square metre, with the price shown online and no yearly contract. 2ACE receives the goods, stores them, picks, packs and ships orders, takes returns, and helps with import and customs. Everything is visible in one dashboard, and calls can be booked in English, Chinese or Arabic.

Our story

We have stood where our customers stand.

We have made products, sold them and run the operations behind them. We know what it feels like when goods leave the factory and then disappear into customs, a courier network or a warehouse where nobody picks up the phone. We also sold online in the EU ourselves, so we know where brands lose money.

2ACE exists to close the gap between the factory and the shelf. It puts the warehouse, the border and the storefront into one plan that a brand builds itself and manages from one dashboard.

China

Where goods are made and sent from

One of us lives in China, close to suppliers and factories, and to the people who ship the goods. Questions about production, dates and what is on the truck are answered by someone who is actually there.

Poland

Where goods are stored and delivered from

The other lives in Poland, on the ground in the EU: your space, receiving, customs paperwork, packing and shipping. When something needs a person to walk over and look, that person is nearby.

How we sound

Plain, direct, specific.

We talk like someone who runs the floor and has nothing to hide. We use numbers, short sentences and everyday words. We never promise what we cannot show.

Say

  • "Storage from 300 zł per m² a month."
  • "Same-day dispatch on a 15:00 cut-off."
  • "Every unit on one screen."
  • "A person replies within one working day."
  • "Space, floor, plan, dashboard."

Avoid

  • "Revolutionary", "world-class", "cutting-edge".
  • "End-to-end solution", "seamless", "synergy".
  • Big numbers with nothing behind them.
  • Names of customers or people.
  • Anything that sounds like a sales call.

Style of the pictures and motion

  • Clean, wide shots of racks, docks and packing tables. Natural light. No stock-photo smiles.
  • Big numbers and short words on screen: "300 zł", "15:00", "48 h".
  • The website walks the floor as you scroll. In video, a slow forward walk along an aisle is the signature shot.
  • Screens are shown at phone and laptop size with sample data, and the amber button is the only bright colour.

Images. The pictures on the website are artwork made for the scroll animation. Use real footage of the real space in ads, and check the facts in the "Before you publish" list of the other document.

Do not

  • Do not name the partners or any customer. The company is presented without people's names.
  • Do not invent years of experience, customer numbers, awards or testimonials. Use only the facts in these two documents.
  • Do not mention a Polish-language version (there is none) or 2ACE Market (it is switched off).
