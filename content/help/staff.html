First sign-in

  1. An admin invites you from Staff. You get an email with a link.
  2. Click the link and choose a password (8 characters or more).
  3. Admin and Support accounts then set up a second factor: scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or 1Password, type the 6-digit code, and you are in.
  4. From then on, signing in asks for your password and a fresh 6-digit code.
Example

You invite marta@2ace.pl as Warehouse. She sets her password and goes straight to the panel with no code. You invite piotr@2ace.pl as Support. After his password he scans the QR code and from then on types a code at each sign-in.

Good to know

If someone is deactivated, their access stops at once. A role change takes effect at their next request.

3. Customers

A list of every company with its owner, plan, status, domain and open requests. Search by company, owner, email or domain, and filter by status.

Open a customer

Opening a customer needs a reason. You choose one (Support request, Billing check, Domain order, Onboarding help, Data correction or Other), add details, and the system records who opened which company and why. You are asked once per customer per browser session.

Example

Anna writes that she never got her invoice. Piotr opens Acme Home, chooses Billing check, and the customer page loads.

What the customer page shows

  • Company: country, tax ID, address, voivodeship, phone, Stripe customer id, join date.
  • People: members and their roles.
  • Plan: the current plan, monthly and one-time amounts, and history of earlier plans.
  • Subscription: Stripe subscription id, status and renewal date.
  • Invoices: loaded live from Stripe.
  • Signed agreements: who signed, which version, from which IP and when. Domain customers also have a "hostinger-domain-registration" row. Keep this evidence at least 3 years, as Hostinger requires.
  • Domain orders and Requests for that company.

Invoices and resending

  1. On the customer page find the Invoices card.
  2. Use View for the customer's invoice page, Stripe to open it in your Stripe dashboard, or Email to owner to send the invoice link to the company owner.
Example

Piotr clicks Email to owner next to invoice INV-0007. A toast says "Sent to anna@acmehome.pl", and the audit log records it.

Change a customer's status (admin only)

Click Change status, choose pending, active, past_due or canceled, and write a reason. This only changes the label in our system. It does not touch Stripe or the subscription.

Example

A test account was marked active by mistake. You set it to pending with the reason "Test account, never paid".

4. Requests inbox

Every message from the website contact form or a customer's Contact us button becomes a request with a thread.

StatusMeaning
newNobody has handled it.
openBeing worked on, or the customer replied.
waitingWe replied and are waiting for the customer.
resolvedDone.

Handle a request

  1. Open the request. Set the priority (low, normal, high) and assign it to a team member. They get an email with a link.
  2. Type a message and choose Reply to email the customer, Note to leave an internal comment the customer never sees, or Log incoming to paste in something the customer wrote by email.
  3. Replying sets the status to waiting. When it is dealt with, set it to resolved.
Example

Request "Import and customs quote" from Acme, priority high. Piotr assigns it to himself, adds the note Ask Kasia for sea freight rates (internal), then replies: Hi Anna, a 20 ft container from Ningbo to Gdansk costs about… Which Incoterm do you use? The status becomes waiting. When Anna answers by email, Piotr pastes her reply with Log incoming and the status returns to open.

Good to know

Replies come from hello@2ace.pl, and the customer can answer to the same address. Customer change requests do not come here: they go to Approvals.

5. Domain queue

Each .pl domain a customer orders (included with a storefront, or paid 99 zł) lands here. By default you register it by hand. Automatic registration through Hostinger is off until you decide to switch it on.

StatusMeaning
pendingNeeds registering.
registeredDone. The customer was emailed once.
failedRegistration did not work. Add a note.
cancelledWill not be registered, for example a refund.

Register a domain by hand

  1. Open the order. It shows the domain and the registrant details (company, tax ID, address, owner email), each with a Copy button.
  2. In your Hostinger account, register the domain in the customer's company name, copying the details from the page.
  3. Back in the panel, paste Hostinger's order reference into the order and click Mark registered. The customer gets the "Your domain is registered" email.
Example

Order: acmehome-sklep.pl, pending. Piotr copies the company name and tax ID, registers it at Hostinger, pastes reference HST-88231, and clicks Mark registered. Anna receives "Your domain acmehome-sklep.pl is registered".

If it does not work

Use Mark failed with a note (for example "Registry rejected the tax ID format") or Cancel. If automatic registration is on, Retry auto-register tries again and tells you the result.

Good to know

The customer accepted Hostinger's agreement before ordering. You can see that on their customer page under Signed agreements.

6. Staff management

Admins only. The screen lists every staff member with role and status.

  1. Click Invite, enter an email and choose a role (admin, support or warehouse).
  2. To change a role or switch someone off, use the buttons on their row.
Rules

You cannot change your own role or deactivate yourself. There must always be at least one active admin.

Example

Marta changes from warehouse to admin because she covers for you. You open Staff, change her role, and the change is in the audit log as staff.update.

7. Audit log

A permanent record of who did what, when and why. Entries cannot be edited or deleted, even by an admin.

It records, among others: opening a customer, status changes, staff changes, domain updates, request replies, locations, products, deliveries, stock moves and adjustments, discrepancies, approvals, photos and invoice emails. Each entry has the person, their role, the action, the item, the company and the reason. You can filter the list.

Example

A customer asks "who changed my delivery?". You filter by Acme and see: change.request by Anna (customer), then change.approve by Marta (warehouse) and inbound.update, with times.

8. Set up the warehouse

Do this once. A location is a labelled place in the warehouse. Its code is what the barcode label says.

TypeUseShared or dedicated
receivingWhere goods land before they are put away.Shared by all customers
quarantineDamaged goods or goods on hold.Shared
binA shelf bin for stock.Dedicated to one customer
palletA pallet slot for stock.Dedicated to one customer
pack, returns, shippingStations for later order handling.Shared
  1. Open Locations. Click New location, code R1, type receiving.
  2. Create Q1, type quarantine.
  3. Click Create many: starts with A-01-, from 1 to 20, type bin. You get A-01-01 to A-01-20 in one go.
  4. Assign a bin: click Assign on A-01-01 and choose the customer. The Customer column now shows the company.
  5. Tick the locations you want and click Print labels (allow pop-ups). Print the page and stick the barcodes on the shelves.
Example

Acme's plan includes 12 pallets. You create PAL-01 to PAL-12 as pallet locations and assign each to Acme Home. Storage is billed from these assignments.

Rules

A bin or pallet belongs to one customer at a time, and the database refuses to put another company's stock in it.

A location holding stock cannot be released, reassigned or switched off. Move the stock first.

Edit changes the note. The code cannot change, because it is printed on labels. Delete location works only for a location never used. Otherwise use Switch off.

9. Receive a delivery

Follow one delivery from the dock to the shelf. The same steps work on the phone scan page.

Before the truck arrives

The Inbound list shows deliveries that are expected, with customer, date, carrier and units. You can also book one for a customer with Book a delivery: choose the customer, then add products and units. If the customer has no products, the dialog says so and offers Go to Products.

Receive the goods

  1. Open the delivery. Under Receive goods choose the product, the quantity and the condition: Good or Damaged.
  2. Good goods go to the receiving area. Damaged goods go to quarantine. For damaged goods, attach photos (a camera button appears on a phone) and add a note.
  3. Click Receive. Repeat for every product and condition. The delivery becomes Being received.
  4. If a product arrives that was not booked, it is accepted but flagged "unexpected".
  5. When everything is counted, click Close receiving. Differences become discrepancies, and the customer and the team inbox are emailed once.
Example: delivery IN-000012

Booked: MUG-BLUE × 600, MUG-RED × 400. Marta receives MUG-BLUE: 590 Good, then 10 Damaged with two photos and the note "cracked in transit". She receives MUG-RED: 400 Good. She clicks Close receiving.

The result is two discrepancies: short for MUG-BLUE (590 of 600 good) and damaged for MUG-BLUE (10). Anna gets an email listing both, with links to the photos.

Resolve discrepancies

Open Discrepancies. Each open item shows the delivery, customer, product, the issue and booked versus received. Click Resolve and write what was decided.

Example

For the short item Marta writes "Supplier confirmed 10 units never shipped, customer informed". For the damaged item: "Customer will claim from carrier. Kept in quarantine until they decide."

Fix a mistake before anything arrives

While a delivery is still Booked and nothing is received, staff can Edit it directly (date, carrier, tracking, products and units, with a remove button on each line), Delete it, or Cancel delivery. Once goods are received it can no longer be edited or deleted.

10. Put away, count and adjust stock

The Stock screen has three tabs: To put away, All stock and Ledger.

Put goods away

  1. Open Stock → To put away. It lists goods waiting in the receiving area, with a thumbnail of each product.
  2. Click Move on a row. Choose the destination (the customer's own bins, plus pack, returns and quarantine areas) and the quantity.
  3. Click Move. The stock leaves the receiving area and appears in the bin.
Example

Marta moves 590 MUG-BLUE and 400 MUG-RED to bin A-01-01, which is assigned to Acme. Both rows leave the queue. The Locations screen now shows 990 units in A-01-01. The 10 damaged units stay in quarantine Q1 until Acme decides what to do.

Rules

You can only move stock into a bin assigned to that customer. You cannot move more than is free, and you cannot move stock back into the receiving area.

Adjust a count

When a physical count differs from the system, post the difference. On All stock click Adjust on the row, enter the change (not the new total) and a reason.

Example

Marta counts 588 MUG-BLUE in A-01-01 but the system says 590. She enters change -2, reason "Cycle count: two broken, found in bin". The ledger gets one line: -2, adjust.

Rules

A reason is required. Changes above 20 units need an admin. An adjustment cannot make stock negative or take away units reserved for orders.

The ledger

Every change in stock is one line: time, customer, product, location, +/- quantity, reason (receive, putaway, adjust) and note. Lines are never edited or deleted. If a number looks wrong, read the ledger from the bottom up and you will find exactly where it changed. Repeated clicks or scans cannot post the same movement twice.

11. Products and photos (staff)

Open Products to see every customer's catalogue, with thumbnails, barcodes, on hand, unplaced and incoming units. Search by SKU, name or barcode and filter by customer.

  • New product creates a product on behalf of a customer, optionally with photos.
  • Open a product to see details, barcodes, stock by location, a photo gallery and the ledger of the latest 100 movements.
  • Edit changes name, size, weight, HS code, origin and active status. Add barcode adds another code. Delete works only for a product never used.
  • Photos: Add photos (up to 4) and Remove. The first is the thumbnail.
Example

Marta scans a carton that has no barcode on file. She opens MUG-RED, clicks Add barcode, scans it, and from then on scanning works.

12. Approvals

Customers cannot edit or delete existing products and deliveries on their own. They send a request, and nothing changes until you approve it.

  1. The overview card "Changes to approve" shows the count. Open Approvals.
  2. Click a request. You see the customer, the request and the current value next to the requested one.
  3. Click Approve and apply to make the change at once, or write a reason and click Decline. The customer is emailed either way, and a decline reason is shown to them.
Example: approve

Request "Edit delivery IN-000012" from Acme. The detail shows Carrier: DSV → GLS and MUG-RED: 400 → 500. Marta checks the dock plan, clicks Approve and apply, and the delivery now shows 500. Anna gets an email.

Example: decline

Request "Delete delivery IN-000009". Marta sees the pallets are already on the dock, writes "Goods arrived this morning, cannot be deleted", and declines. Anna sees that reason on her delivery.

Rules

If a change can no longer be applied (for example the goods arrived after the request), approving it is refused and the request stays open. Decline it with a reason. A newer request for the same item replaces the older pending one. Staff themselves edit directly and never use this queue.

13. The scan app on a phone

Open 2ace.pl/scan on a phone or handheld scanner. It works for Warehouse accounts, and for admins after the two-step check.

  1. Sign in at 2ace.pl/login with a Warehouse account, then open /scan. The first screen has Receive and Put away.
  2. A hardware scanner types into the big box and presses Enter. On a phone, tap Camera, or type the code and tap OK.

Receive on the phone

  1. Tap Receive and pick the delivery.
  2. Scan or type the product barcode. A card shows the product photo, SKU and name so you can check it is the right item.
  3. Set the quantity, then tap Good or Damaged. For damaged goods, take photos first.
  4. The list below shows progress, for example 590 / 600 good · 10 damaged. Tap Finish delivery when done. If there are differences, the customer is emailed.

Put away on the phone

  1. Tap Put away and scan the product.
  2. The card shows how many are waiting in receiving. Check the quantity.
  3. Scan the bin label, for example A-01-01. The move is posted, and the green message reads "Moved 590 × MUG-BLUE to A-01-01".
Example

Marta scans 5901234123457. The card shows the blue mug photo and "590 waiting at R1". She scans the label on the shelf, A-01-01, and hears the confirmation beep.

Good to know

If you scan a wrong code, a red message tells you what was not found. A repeated scan cannot book stock twice.
