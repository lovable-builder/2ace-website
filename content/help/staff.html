First sign-in

  1. An admin invites you from Staff. You get an email with a link.
  2. Click the link and choose a password (8 characters or more).
  3. Admin and Support accounts then set up a second factor: scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or 1Password, type the 6-digit code, and you are in.
  4. From then on, signing in asks for your password and a fresh 6-digit code.
Example

You invite marta@2ace.pl as Warehouse. She sets her password and goes straight to the panel with no code. You invite piotr@2ace.pl as Support. After his password he scans the QR code and from then on types a code at each sign-in.

Good to know

If someone is deactivated, their access stops at once. A role change takes effect at their next request.

3. Customers

A list of every company with its owner, plan, status, domain and open requests. Search by company, owner, email or domain, and filter by status.

Open a customer

Opening a customer needs a reason. You choose one (Support request, Billing check, Domain order, Onboarding help, Data correction or Other), add details, and the system records who opened which company and why. You are asked once per customer per browser session.

Example

Anna writes that she never got her invoice. Piotr opens Acme Home, chooses Billing check, and the customer page loads.

What the customer page shows

  • Company: country, tax ID, address, voivodeship, phone, Stripe customer id, join date.
  • People: members and their roles.
  • Plan: the current plan, monthly and one-time amounts, and history of earlier plans.
  • Shipping and fulfilment (the box right under Plan): the customer's fulfilment mode (from their plan: Fulfilment, Fulfilment as you go, or storage only), their shipping markup, whether they may buy labels themselves, the most unpaid shipping they may have at once, and the labels waiting to be invoiced. An admin clicks Edit to change these. The mode override is for testing only; normally it follows the plan. The markup is added to the carrier's net price (empty = the default of 30%).
  • Subscription: Stripe subscription id, status and renewal date.
  • Invoices: loaded live from Stripe.
  • Signed agreements: who signed, which version, from which IP and when. Domain customers also have a "hostinger-domain-registration" row. Keep this evidence at least 3 years, as Hostinger requires.
  • Domain orders and Requests for that company.

Where to find it: Customers in the left menu, then click a company's row. Scroll past the Company, People and Plan boxes. The separate Shipping item in the left menu is only the connection test to Furgonetka.

Invoices and resending

  1. On the customer page find the Invoices card.
  2. Use View for the customer's invoice page, Stripe to open it in your Stripe dashboard, or Email to owner to send the invoice link to the company owner.
Example

Piotr clicks Email to owner next to invoice INV-0007. A toast says "Sent to anna@acmehome.pl", and the audit log records it.

Change a customer's status (admin only)

Click Change status, choose pending, active, past_due or canceled, and write a reason. This only changes the label in our system. It does not touch Stripe or the subscription.

Example

A test account was marked active by mistake. You set it to pending with the reason "Test account, never paid".

Handle a request

  1. Open the request. Set the priority (low, normal, high) and assign it to a team member. They get an email with a link.
  2. Type a message and choose Reply to email the customer, Note to leave an internal comment the customer never sees, or Log incoming to paste in something the customer wrote by email.

Example

Request "Import and customs quote" from Acme, priority high. Good to know

Replies come from hello@2ace.pl, and the customer can answer to the same address. Customer change requests do not come here: they go to Approvals.

Register a domain by hand

  1. Open the order. It shows the domain and the registrant details (company, tax ID, address, owner email), each with a Copy button.
  2. In your Hostinger account, register the domain in the customer's company name, copying the details from the page.
  3. Back in the panel, paste Hostinger's order reference into the order and click Mark registered. The customer gets the "Your domain is registered" email.

Order: acmehome-sklep.pl, pending. Piotr copies the company name and tax ID, registers it at Hostinger, pastes reference HST-88231, and clicks Mark registered. Anna receives "Your domain acmehome-sklep.pl is registered".

If it does not work

Use Mark failed with a note (for example "Registry rejected the tax ID format") or Cancel. If automatic registration is on, Retry auto-register tries again and tells you the result.

The customer accepted Hostinger's agreement before ordering. You can see that on their customer page under Signed agreements.

7. Audit log

A permanent record of who did what, when and why. Entries cannot be edited or deleted, even by an admin.

It records, among others: opening a customer, status changes, staff changes, domain updates, request replies, locations, products, deliveries, stock moves and adjustments, discrepancies, approvals, photos and invoice emails. Each entry has the person, their role, the action, the item, the company and the reason. You can filter the list.

Example

A customer asks "who changed my delivery?". You filter by Acme and see: change.request by Anna (customer), then change.approve by Marta (warehouse) and inbound.update, with times.

8. Locations

A location is a labelled place in the warehouse. Its code is what the barcode label says. You do not have to set anything up before your first delivery: the places the system needs are created the first time they are used.

Use Locations yourself when you want to lay the space out: extra bins or pallet slots for a customer, or printed labels for the shelves.

  1. Open Locations. Click New location, give a code such as A-01-01 and a type.
  2. Or click Create many: starts with A-01-, from 1 to 20, type bin. You get A-01-01 to A-01-20 in one go.
  3. Assign a bin or pallet to a customer: click Assign and choose the customer. The Customer column shows the company.
  4. Tick the locations you want and click Print labels (allow pop-ups). Print the page and stick the barcodes on the shelves.
Example

Acme's plan is 15 m². You mark out that space in the park as pallet slots, create PAL-01 to PAL-12 and assign each to Acme Home. The plan is sold by the square metre; the bins and pallet slots are how you lay that space out. Without doing this, Acme's goods would still go into an automatic bin called ACMEHOME-01.

Rules

A bin or pallet belongs to one customer at a time, and the database refuses to put another company's stock in it.

A location holding stock cannot be released, reassigned or switched off. Move the stock first.

Edit changes the note. The code cannot change, because it is printed on labels. Delete location works only for a location never used. Otherwise use Switch off.

9. Receive a delivery

Follow one delivery from the dock to the shelf. The same steps work on the phone scan page.

Before the truck arrives

The Inbound list shows deliveries that are expected, with customer, date, carrier and units. You can also book one for a customer with Book a delivery: choose the customer, then add products and units. If the customer has no products, the dialog says so and offers Go to Products.

Receive the goods

  1. Open the delivery. Under Receive goods choose the product, the quantity and the condition: Good or Damaged.
  2. Good goods go straight into the customer's own bin and are available at once. The bin is created and assigned automatically the first time, so there is nothing to set up. Damaged goods go to quarantine: attach photos (a camera button appears on a phone) and add a note.
  3. Click Receive. Repeat for every product and condition. The delivery becomes Being received.
  4. If a product arrives that was not booked, it is accepted but flagged "unexpected". It waits in the receiving area until someone decides.
  5. When everything is counted, click Close receiving. Differences become discrepancies, and the customer and the team inbox are emailed once.
Example: delivery IN-000012

Booked: MUG-BLUE × 600, MUG-RED × 400. Marta receives MUG-BLUE: 590 Good, then 10 Damaged with two photos and the note "cracked in transit". She receives MUG-RED: 400 Good. She clicks Close receiving.

The result is two discrepancies: short for MUG-BLUE (590 of 600 good) and damaged for MUG-BLUE (10). Anna gets an email listing both, with links to the photos.

Resolve discrepancies

Open Discrepancies. Each open item shows the delivery, customer, product, the issue and booked versus received. Click Resolve and write what was decided.

Example

For the short item Marta writes "Supplier confirmed 10 units never shipped, customer informed". For the damaged item: "Customer will claim from carrier. Kept in quarantine until they decide."

Fix a mistake before anything arrives

While a delivery is still Booked and nothing is received, staff can Edit it directly (date, carrier, tracking, products and units, with a remove button on each line), Delete it, or Cancel delivery. Once goods are received it can no longer be edited or deleted.

10. Add stock, move, count and adjust

The Stock screen has three tabs: All stock, To put away and Ledger. Most days you only use All stock.

Add stock in one step

For opening stock, a recount of a new customer, or test stock, you do not need to book and receive a delivery.

  1. Open Stock and click Add stock.
  2. Choose the customer, then the product (only their active products are listed), the units, and an optional note such as "Opening stock".
  3. Click Add stock. The units go into the customer's own bin (created automatically if they have none) and can be ordered immediately.
Example

Acme Home already has 40 MUG-BLUE on a pallet at the park. Marta opens Add stock, chooses Acme Home and MUG-BLUE, enters 40 and the note "Opening stock". The Ledger shows one line, +40, adjust, with that note, and Anna can order the mugs straight away.

Move stock between places

Click Move on a row of All stock to move units to another of the customer's bins, or to the pack, returns or quarantine areas.

Goods waiting in the receiving area

Good goods normally never wait there. To put away only lists goods that arrived unexpectedly (not on the booking), or all received goods if automatic storing has been switched off. Two ways to deal with a row, without the scanner:

  • Store in bin: one click. All the free units go into the customer's own bin, which is created and assigned automatically if they have none. A message says where they went.
  • Move: choose the destination and the quantity yourself. The first choice is the customer's own bin (automatic); the others are their assigned bins and the pack, returns and quarantine areas.
Example

Marta finds 12 MUG-TEAL waiting in RECEIVING. She opens Stock, finds the row, clicks Store in bin, and sees "Stored 12 in ACMEHOME-01". The mugs are available to order at once. Had she only wanted 5 there, she would have used Move and typed 5.

Rules

You can only move stock into a bin assigned to that customer. You cannot move more than is free, and you cannot move stock back into the receiving area. Damaged goods in quarantine are not available until someone moves them.

Adjust a count

When a physical count differs from the system, post the difference. On All stock click Adjust on the row, enter the change (not the new total) and a reason.

Example

Marta counts 588 MUG-BLUE in A-01-01 but the system says 590. She enters change -2, reason "Cycle count: two broken, found in bin". The ledger gets one line: -2, adjust.

Rules

A reason is required. Changes above 20 units need an admin. An adjustment cannot make stock negative or take away units reserved for orders.

The ledger

Every change in stock is one line: time, customer, product, location, +/- quantity, reason (receive, putaway, adjust) and note. Lines are never edited or deleted. If a number looks wrong, read the ledger from the bottom up and you will find exactly where it changed. Repeated clicks or scans cannot post the same movement twice.

11. Products and photos (staff)

Open Products to see every customer's catalogue, with thumbnails, barcodes, on hand, unplaced and incoming units. Search by SKU, name or barcode and filter by customer.

  • New product creates a product on behalf of a customer, optionally with photos.
  • Open a product to see details, barcodes, stock by location, a photo gallery and the ledger of the latest 100 movements.
  • Edit changes name, size, weight, HS code, origin and active status. Add barcode adds another code. Delete works only for a product never used.
  • Photos: Add photos (up to 4) and Remove. The first is the thumbnail.
Example

Marta scans a carton that has no barcode on file. She opens MUG-RED, clicks Add barcode, scans it, and from then on scanning works.

12. Approvals

Customers cannot edit or delete existing products and deliveries on their own. They send a request, and nothing changes until you approve it.

  1. The overview card "Changes to approve" shows the count. Open Approvals.
  2. Click a request. You see the customer, the request and the current value next to the requested one.
  3. Click Approve and apply to make the change at once, or write a reason and click Decline. The customer is emailed either way, and a decline reason is shown to them.
Example: approve

Request "Edit delivery IN-000012" from Acme. The detail shows Carrier: DSV → GLS and MUG-RED: 400 → 500. Marta checks the dock plan, clicks Approve and apply, and the delivery now shows 500. Anna gets an email.

Example: decline

Request "Delete delivery IN-000009". Marta sees the pallets are already on the dock, writes "Goods arrived this morning, cannot be deleted", and declines. Anna sees that reason on her delivery.

Rules

If a change can no longer be applied (for example the goods arrived after the request), approving it is refused and the request stays open. Decline it with a reason. A newer request for the same item replaces the older pending one. Staff themselves edit directly and never use this queue.

13. Orders and reserved stock

Customers place orders (by hand or from a CSV file). Each order is reserved as a whole, or held with a reason. You see them under Orders.

How reserving works

  • An order reserves stock only if every line can be filled. Otherwise nothing is reserved and the order is on hold, with text such as MUG-BLUE: need 7, available 6. The customer is emailed once.
  • Only stock in bins and pallets counts. Goods in the receiving area (unexpected deliveries) or in quarantine are not for sale until someone moves them to a bin.
  • Stock is taken earliest expiry first, then by location code. Reserved units cannot be moved away or removed by a count.
  • Held orders are retried automatically, oldest first, whenever stock is put away, a count adds stock, or an order is cancelled.
Example

Acme has 10 MUG-BLUE in bin A-01-01. Order ORD-000001 takes 4. ORD-000002 asks for 7 and goes on hold (6 free). Marta puts 5 more mugs away into A-01-01. ORD-000002 is reserved automatically and Acme sees Reserved, waiting to be picked.

The Orders screen

Filter by status or customer. Open an order to see the recipient, the lines and exactly which bin each line is reserved in. On a held order, Try to reserve now retries it at once (for example after you found stock). New order places an order for a customer, such as one received by phone.

Cancelling

Staff cancel directly with Cancel order: the reservations are released and held orders get another go. A customer's cancellation arrives under Approvals and is applied when you approve it. An order that is already being picked cannot be cancelled here.

Rules

Support can see orders but not change them. A customer's own order number is unique per customer, so importing the same file twice never duplicates orders.

Delivery details that will not pass the label

Open an order and look at the Ship to card. If the name, phone, postal code or street would be refused by the carrier, a red box lists every problem in plain words (for example "The recipient phone must be a Polish number with 9 digits"). The same box shows the carrier's reason when a label attempt failed.

  1. Click Edit details to correct them yourself. The same rules apply as for a new order, and a refusal is shown inside the form. Phone, postal code and country are tidied automatically.
  2. Or click Ask the customer to correct it. The text is filled in from the problems; change it if you like and click Send email. The owner of the customer account gets an email with the problem and the current details, and the order shows the same note in their Orders tab.
  3. When they reply, click Edit details and save. That clears their note. Then create the label again.

You can edit until a label is bought; once it is, the details are locked because the label would no longer match. Nothing is charged for a failed attempt. Admin and Warehouse can edit and ask; Support can only see the problems.

14. Picking and packing

A reserved order is picked from the shelves, checked and packed on the phone scan page. Every step moves real stock in the ledger and records the parcels.

The packing station

Picked items go to a packing station, PACK-1. It is created automatically the first time you pick, so there is nothing to set up.

Pick

  1. On /scan tap Pick and choose an order. Lines are listed in bin order, so you walk the floor once.
  2. The screen shows the bin to go to (for example A-01-01), the product, its photo and the quantity. Nothing to scan or type.
  3. Take the items and tap Picked 4. If you want to double-check, scan the bin or the item: it says "Right bin", "Right item" or names the mistake, but it never blocks you.
  4. The reservation becomes a real movement: The reservation becomes a real movement: the units leave the bin and arrive at the packing station. A repeated tap never moves them twice.
  5. When the last line is done, tap Pack this order.
Example

ORD-000001 needs MUG-BLUE × 4 from bin A-01-01 and MUG-RED × 2 from A-02-01. Marta walks to A-01-01, takes 4 blue mugs and taps "Picked 4", then does the same for A-02-01. Bin A-01-01 now holds 4 fewer and PACK-1 holds 4 blue and 2 red mugs.

Pack

  1. Tap Pack and choose a picked order. The screen shows who it ships to.
  2. Scan every item going into the parcel. An item that is not on the order is refused, and so is a scan beyond the ordered quantity. For an item with no barcode tap +1 (no barcode).
  3. When everything is counted, weigh and measure each parcel: weight in kg, length, width and height in cm. Use Add another parcel for a second box.
  4. Tap Finish packing. The order becomes packed with its parcels recorded, ready for its shipping label.
Example

Marta scans 4 blue and 2 red mugs, puts them in one box that weighs 1.8 kg and measures 30 × 20 × 15 cm, and taps Finish. The order shows Packed in the admin panel with that parcel.

When something is wrong

If a bin is empty or an item is damaged, tap Can't find it, say what is wrong, and tap Stop this order. The whole order goes on hold, every reservation is released and the reason is shown on the order. Then:

  1. Return anything already picked from the packing station to its shelf: Stock → All stock → Move, from PACK-1 back to the bin. The held order is retried automatically when stock lands in a bin.
  2. Count the bin that was wrong and fix it with Adjust, so the system matches the shelf.
Rules

Only warehouse staff and admins pick and pack. An order that has started picking can no longer be cancelled. A packed order cannot be picked again.

15. Shipping labels

When an order is packed, you buy its label here. The carriers are compared for the exact parcels, you choose one, and the order becomes shipped.

Buy a label

  1. Open Orders and click a packed order. The Shipping label card shows when it is ready.
  2. Click Get shipping prices. This is free. You see each carrier with what we pay and what the customer pays (our price plus the markup, before VAT). Carriers that cannot take the parcel are listed with the reason.
  3. Click Buy label on the one you want and read the question. In the test environment nothing real is charged; in the live one it spends from the Furgonetka balance.
  4. Confirm. The order becomes shipped, the goods leave the stock, and the card shows the carrier, the tracking numbers and what the customer is charged.
  5. Click Download label and print it. Stick it on the parcel.
Example

ORD-000001 is one parcel of 1,8 kg. DPD costs us 12,50 zł net, so the customer is charged 16,25 zł net plus VAT. InPost shows "not available: Parcel too heavy". Marta buys the DPD label, prints it and hands the parcel to the courier.

Automatic labels

When an admin has switched automatic labels on, you do not buy the label yourself: after you tap Finish packing on the scan page the system buys the cheapest carrier that can take the parcel. The screen then says one of these:

  • "Label bought: DPD…, tracking …. Print it from the admin panel." Open the order in Orders and click Download label. The order is already marked shipped.
  • "Ready for its label" (nothing else): automatic labels are off. Use Get shipping prices in the admin as before.
  • "No label created automatically: …" in red: the packing is saved, but the label stopped (for example the label is above the limit or the balance is low). Read the reason, then use Get shipping prices or Create the cheapest label on the order.
  • If the customer brought their own label, nothing is bought and the screen says so.

It tries up to three of the cheapest carriers if one refuses the parcel before any charge. It never buys twice: if a carrier does not confirm in time, it stops and an alert goes to the team.

Example

Marta weighs ORD-000021 at 1,2 kg, 30 × 20 × 15 cm, and taps Finish packing. A few seconds later the phone says "Label bought: DPD · package, tracking WB123". She goes to her computer, opens the order, clicks Download label, prints and sticks it.

When the customer brought their own label

If the customer added their own label, the order's Shipping label card shows it instead of prices: carrier, tracking numbers, the file and "Charged: Nothing".

  1. Click Open the PDF to print and print it (the link works for two minutes). A tracking-only label has no file; write the tracking number on the parcel if the carrier needs it.
  2. Pick and pack the order as usual.
  3. When it is packed, click Mark shipped. The goods leave the stock and the order is shipped.

You cannot buy a label for an order while the customer's own label is on it. Ask the customer to remove theirs first.

Example

ORD-000014 has Anna's InPost label. Marta prints it, packs the box, sticks the label on and clicks Mark shipped. Anna is not charged for shipping.

Sender

Labels show the 2ACE warehouse (Box 17, Żwirowa 66, 05-090 Puchały) as the sender, with hello@2ace.pl.

Safety limits

  • Switched off: an admin can turn buying off instantly. Prices still show.
  • Balance: a label is refused if the Furgonetka balance does not cover it. Top up in Furgonetka.
  • Over the limit: a single label above the per-label limit (80 zł by default) needs an admin to buy it and confirm.
  • Daily limit: when the day's labels would pass the daily cap, buying stops for everyone until tomorrow or until an admin raises it.

When something goes wrong

  • "Furgonetka rejected the shipment: …" The address or parcel data is not accepted (often a missing phone or a wrong postal code). Nothing was charged. New orders cannot be created without a first name and surname, a 9-digit Polish phone, a house number and a valid postal code, so this mostly happens with older orders. Open the order: the Ship to card lists what is wrong. Click Edit details to fix it yourself, or Ask the customer to correct it (see section 13).
  • "Waiting to be checked": Furgonetka did not confirm in time. It may have been charged, so do not buy again. Press Check the label order: it records the label if it was bought, or closes the attempt if it was not.
  • Goods no longer at the packing station: someone moved them. Put them back before buying.
Rules

Only warehouse staff and admins buy labels; support can read them. An order can have one label. Cancelling or refunding a label is done in Furgonetka for now. Delivery to a parcel locker or pickup point is not supported yet; labels are for delivery to the address.

16. The scan app on a phone

Open 2ace.pl/scan on a phone or handheld scanner. It works for Warehouse accounts, and for admins after the two-step check. It has four jobs: Receive, Put away, Pick and Pack. Receiving and putting away are described here; picking and packing have their own section (14), and what happens to the label after packing is in section 15.

  1. Sign in at 2ace.pl/login with a Warehouse account, then open /scan. The first screen has four big buttons: Receive, Put away, Pick and Pack.
  2. A hardware scanner types into the big box and presses Enter. On a phone, tap Camera, or type the code and tap OK.

Receive on the phone

  1. Tap Receive and pick the delivery.
  2. Scan or type the product barcode. A card shows the product photo, SKU and name so you can check it is the right item.
  3. Set the quantity, then tap Good or Damaged. For damaged goods, take photos first.
  4. The list below shows progress, for example 590 / 600 good · 10 damaged. Tap Finish delivery when done. If there are differences, the customer is emailed.

Good goods from a booked delivery are stored in the customer's bin the moment you tap Good, and the green message says where ("Stored in ACMEHOME-01. Available now"). You only need Put away for goods that were not on the booking, or when automatic storing is switched off.

Put away on the phone

  1. Tap Put away and scan the product.
  2. The card shows how many are waiting in the receiving area. Check the quantity.
  3. Scan the bin label, for example A-01-01. The move is posted, and the green message reads "Moved 590 × MUG-BLUE to A-01-01".
Example

Marta scans 5901234123457. The card shows the blue mug photo and "590 waiting at R1". She scans the label on the shelf, A-01-01, and hears the confirmation beep.

Good to know

If you scan a wrong code, a red message tells you what was not found. A repeated scan cannot book stock twice.

18. Booked calls (Appointments)

Anyone, customer or guest, can book a call at 2ace.pl/book in English, Chinese or Arabic. Admin and Support manage the bookings under Appointments.

What happens when someone books

  • The customer gets an email in the language they chose, with the time in their own time zone, a calendar file, an "add to Google Calendar" link and a link to move or cancel.
  • The people set for that language, and hello@2ace.pl, get an email with the name, contact details, topic and the time in their time zone and the customer's. If the meeting link is not set yet, the email says so.
  • 24 hours and 1 hour before, the customer and the team get a reminder. A booking made less than 24 hours ahead skips the 24 hour reminder.

Send the meeting link

  1. Open Appointments and click the booking. The list shows a red no link for calls without a link.
  2. Paste the Google Meet, Zoom or Teams link (it must start with https://).
  3. Click Save and email the link to send it to the customer at once, in their language, or Save link to keep it for the reminders. A link saved before a reminder is included in that reminder.

An admin can also set a default meeting link per language under Booking settings. Every new booking in that language then gets it automatically.

Opening hours and languages (admin)

Under Booking settings each meeting language has its own block: Open for booking, the time zone of the person taking the calls (for example Asia/Shanghai or Europe/Warsaw), who is emailed, the call length, a break between calls, minimum notice, how far ahead people can book, and the opening times per weekday written like 10:00-14:00, 15:00-18:00 (empty = closed). A language stays closed, and hidden from the booking page, until you switch it on with at least one opening time and one email address.

Example

The Chinese-language calls are taken from Shanghai on Monday and Wednesday, 10:00 to 12:00 local time. In Booking settings → Chinese the admin sets the time zone Asia/Shanghai, Monday 10:00-12:00, Wednesday 10:00-12:00, adds the partner's email and ticks Open for booking. A visitor in Warsaw sees those slots as 04:00 to 06:00 and a visitor in Shanghai sees 10:00 to 12:00. Li Wei books Monday 10:00 Shanghai time; the partner gets the email, pastes a Zoom link into the booking and clicks Save and email the link.

Good to know

The top of the page says whether reminders are running. If it says they are not, ask the owner to check the reminder job (owner guide). Nobody can book a time that is outside the opening hours, inside the minimum notice, or already taken.
