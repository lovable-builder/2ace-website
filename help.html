How to use your 2ACE account, with an example for every step.

From creating your account to booking a delivery and reading your stock. The examples follow one made-up company, Acme Home Sp. z o.o., so you can see each step with real numbers. Stuck on something? Write to hello@2ace.pl or call +48 608 180 946.

On this page

1. Create your account

Everything starts at 2ace.pl/login. You create one login for your company, then build a plan and pay.

  1. Open Log in and choose the Create account tab.
  2. Fill in your details: full name, email, phone, company name, country, tax or VAT ID, address, postal code and city. If your country is Poland, you also pick your voivodeship.
  3. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and click Create account.
  4. Open the email we send and click the confirmation link. It brings you back to the login page.
  5. Log in with your email and password.
Example

Anna fills in: Anna Kowalska, anna@acmehome.pl, +48 600 100 200, company Acme Home Sp. z o.o., country Poland, NIP 5252000000, voivodeship Mazowieckie, ul. Prosta 1, 00-001 Warszawa. After clicking the link in her email she logs in and lands in her dashboard.

If you forget your password

On the login page type your email, then click Forgot password?. We email you a link to choose a new one. You can also change your password any time under Account.

Your account page

Open 2ace.pl/account from the menu. You can edit your personal details, your company details, upload a company logo (it appears as your avatar), see invoices, open billing, and change your password.

Example

Anna uploads acme-logo.png under Company logo. From then on, her dashboard header shows the logo and "Welcome, Acme Home Sp. z o.o.".

2. Build your plan

You choose what you need in four steps and see the monthly price update as you go. Nothing is charged until you sign and pay.

Step 1: Space

Space is what you store goods in. You pick shelf bins or pallets, and how many.

Space typePrice per monthAreaRange
Shelf bin90 zł0.3 m²1 to 2,000 bins
Pallet360 zł1.2 m²1 to 1,000 pallets

Both work out to 300 zł per m² a month. You type the number or drag the slider.

Step 2: Services

  • E-commerce fulfillment: we pick, pack and ship your orders. 350 zł per m² of your space, per month.
  • Returns handling: we receive, grade and restock returned items. 150 zł per m² of your space, per month.
  • Import and customs: sea, air and road freight with customs cleared into Poland. It is quoted for each shipment, so it adds nothing to the monthly price.

Step 3: Sell

  • .pl storefront: we build and host your online shop. 199 zł per month plus a 2,950 zł one-time setup. The setup includes your .pl domain, so you choose a name here and we check it is free.
  • 2ACE Market: sell on our marketplace with no setup and no monthly fee. You pay a commission only when something sells: 3.6% to 9.2% depending on category.
Market categoryCommission per sale

Beauty and health6.4%
Fashion9.2%
Kids7.1%
Tech accessories3.6%

Step 4: Review, sign and pay

  1. Check the lines and the totals. All prices are net, without VAT.
  2. Sign in or create your account if you have not yet. Your plan is saved while you do.
  3. Read the service agreement summary, tick the box and type your full name to sign. If your plan has a storefront, signing also accepts Hostinger's domain registration agreement, because your .pl domain is registered through Hostinger in your company's name.
  4. Click Continue to secure payment. You pay on Stripe's page with a card, Apple Pay, Przelewy24 or SEPA.
  5. When the payment clears, your plan goes live. This usually takes a few seconds.

4. Add and manage products

Every product you want us to store needs an entry before you can book a delivery of it.

Add a product

  1. Open Products and click Add a product.
  2. Enter a SKU (your own code, up to 60 characters) and a name. The barcode (EAN) is optional but speeds up scanning at our warehouse.
  3. Add up to 4 photos. The first one becomes the thumbnail in your lists.
  4. Click Save product. It shows at once, with 0 in stock.
Example

SKU MUG-BLUE, name Blue ceramic mug, barcode 5901234123457, two photos. Then the same for MUG-RED. Each SKU can be used once in your account. Another company can use the same SKU for its own product.

Change or remove a product

New products appear immediately. Changes to products that already exist are requests that our team approves, so your catalogue stays reliable for the warehouse.

  1. On the product's row click Request edit.
  2. Change the name, add another barcode, or choose Request switch off or Request delete.
  3. Click Send for approval. The row now says "Waiting for approval". You can click Cancel request any time before we decide.
  4. We approve or decline. You get an email either way. If declined, the reason shows on the row.
Example

Anna renames MUG-BLUE to "Blue ceramic mug 350 ml". The row shows "Waiting for approval: Edit product MUG-BLUE". An hour later Piotr's colleague approves it and Anna gets an email "Approved: Edit product MUG-BLUE". The new name shows in her list.

Rules

A product that was ever on a delivery or in stock cannot be deleted. Ask us to switch it off instead. It stays in your history and no longer appears when you book deliveries.

Photos are the exception: you add or remove product photos yourself, with no approval, because they only describe the product. In the edit panel use Add photos or Remove. A product holds at most 4 photos.

5. Book and follow a delivery

An inbound delivery is goods on their way to our warehouse. Book it before it arrives so the team knows what to expect.

Book a delivery

  1. Open Inbound and click Book a delivery. Your plan must be active and you need at least one product.
  2. Set the expected arrival date, the carrier and a tracking number if you have one.
  3. Under "What is arriving" choose a product, enter the units and click Add. Repeat for each product. Each product can appear once per delivery.
  4. Click Book delivery.
Example

Expected 10 Nov 2026, carrier DSV, tracking DSV-4821937. Lines: MUG-BLUE × 600 and MUG-RED × 400. The delivery appears as IN-000012, status Booked, 1,000 units.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaning
BookedWe know it is coming. Nothing has arrived yet. You can still request changes.
Being receivedThe goods are at our dock and being counted.
ReceivedCounting is finished and the delivery is closed.
CancelledThe delivery was cancelled.

Change or delete a booked delivery

While a delivery is still Booked, click Request edit to change the date, carrier, tracking, products or units, or Request delete to remove it. Like product changes, these are sent for approval and the delivery stays as booked until we approve. Once goods have arrived, a delivery can no longer be changed.

Example

The supplier can only send 500 MUG-RED. Anna clicks Request edit on IN-000012, changes MUG-RED from 400 to 500, and clicks Send for approval. She sees "Waiting for approval: Edit delivery IN-000012".

When something is different on arrival

If fewer or more units arrive than you booked, goods arrive damaged, or an item was not on the booking, we record a difference. You see a red line on the delivery, and we email you.

Example

Marta counts 590 good MUG-BLUE and 10 cracked. Anna's delivery now shows: Differences found: MUG-BLUE: 590 of 600 arrived · MUG-BLUE: 10 damaged, with links Photo 1 and Photo 2 showing the damage. The photo links stay valid for an hour each time you open the page.

Inventory lists every product that has stock or units on the way, with a thumbnail.

Awaiting put-awayReceived, but not yet moved to your bin (still in the receiving area), or held in quarantine because they were damaged.

Example

Right after Marta receives IN-000012 and before she puts goods on the shelf, Anna sees MUG-BLUE: on hand 600, available 600, awaiting put-away 600, incoming 0. After put-away, awaiting put-away drops to 10 (the damaged units in quarantine) and the rest sits on her shelf.

Good to know

You only ever see your own stock. The numbers come straight from our stock ledger, where every unit that moves is recorded.

Place and follow orders

Orders are the parcels you want us to send to your buyers. We reserve the stock for each order the moment you place it.

Place one order

  1. Open Orders and click New order. Your plan must be active and your products must be on our shelves.
  2. Enter your own order number (optional but recommended), the recipient's name, address, postal code, city and a two-letter country code such as PL or DE. Email and phone are optional.
  3. Choose each product and quantity and click Add. The list shows how many of each you have available.
  4. Click Place order.
Example

Anna places order SHOP-1001 for Jan Nowak, Prosta 1, 00-001 Warszawa, PL: MUG-BLUE × 2 and MUG-RED × 1. We have both on the shelf, so the order shows Reserved, waiting to be picked straight away.

All of it or none of it

We reserve an order only when every line can be filled. If one line is short, nothing is reserved, the order goes On hold with the reason, and we email you. It is reserved automatically when more stock is put away on our shelves, oldest orders first. Stock that is still in our receiving area, or in quarantine, cannot be sold yet.

Example

Anna orders MUG-BLUE × 7 and MUG-RED × 1, but only 6 blue mugs are on the shelf. The order is on hold: MUG-BLUE: need 7, available 6. The red mug is not reserved either. When her next delivery is put away, the order is reserved by itself.

Import many orders from a CSV file

  1. Click Import CSV and Download the template.
  2. Fill one row per product. Rows with the same order_ref become one order. Match products by sku. Excel files saved with semicolons work too.
  3. Choose the file. You see how many orders are ready. Click Import orders.
  4. Every order gets its own result line: reserved, on hold, already imported, or the exact reason it failed. The good ones are kept even if others fail.
Example

A file with SHOP-1001 (two rows) and SHOP-1002 imports as two orders. Importing the same file again creates nothing new: both come back as "Already imported".

Cancel an order

Click Request cancellation. Like other changes, it needs our approval, and you get an email with the result. Once picking has started an order can no longer be cancelled here.

7. Change your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade yourself. We show the difference first and you confirm once.

  1. Click Build plan in the top bar. A banner says "You already have an active plan". Click Change plan. The same button is on the Storefront and 2ACE Market tabs. The builder opens with your current plan.
  2. Change what you need and go to the Review step. A badge shows Upgrade, Downgrade or No price change.
  3. Read the sentence about what is charged today and your new monthly price. Click Confirm.

Three examples

Upgrade

Acme has 12 pallets at 4,320 zł and adds fulfillment: the monthly price becomes 9,360 zł, a difference of 5,040 zł. With half of the billing month left, the review says: Charged today: about 2,520 zł. From your next invoice you pay 9,360 zł a month. The card on file is charged straight away, and the invoice is emailed.

Downgrade

Acme goes from 12 to 6 pallets: 4,320 zł becomes 2,160 zł. With half the month left, the review says: You get a credit of about 1,080 zł on your next invoice. Nothing is charged today.

No price change

Acme adds 2ACE Market or Import and customs. They have no monthly price (a commission per sale, a quote per shipment), so the badge says "No price change" and the review says "Nothing is charged today". Your plan is updated and your monthly price stays the same.

Good to know

Adding a storefront needs a .pl domain name and adds the one-time 2,950 zł setup. If the card payment fails, the plan is not changed and you are not charged.

8. Get a .pl domain

If your active plan has no domain yet, a New badge appears on the Domain tab.

  1. Open Domain. Type the name you want, without .pl, and click Check. We check it against the Polish registry.
  2. If it is taken, suggested alternatives appear. Click one to check it.
  3. Tick the box to accept Hostinger's Domain Name Registration Agreement. We register your domain through Hostinger, in your company's name.
  4. If your plan has a storefront the domain is included: click Request my included domain. Otherwise click Pay 99 zł and request. The 99 zł is one time, net, and covers the first year.
  5. We register the domain and email you within one working day. The tab then shows the domain and its status.
Example

Anna types acmehome. The answer: acmehome.pl is already registered. Try: and two suggestions. She clicks acmehome-sklep.pl, which looks available, ticks the box, pays 99 zł on Stripe, and sees "Payment received. We are registering your domain".

9. Invoices and payments

  • Every payment sends you an email with a link to the invoice and a PDF.
  • Your invoices are listed under Account → Billing and invoices, each with a link to view or download it.
  • Manage payment method and subscription opens Stripe's secure portal, where you update your card or see your subscription.
  • If a payment fails we email you. Update your card in the portal to keep your plan active.
Example

Anna's card expires. The renewal fails and she gets "Payment failed: action needed". She opens Account, clicks Manage payment method and subscription, adds a new card, and the next attempt succeeds.

Good to know

Invoices come from our payment provider and carry our company details. All plan prices are quoted without VAT.
