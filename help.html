How to use your 2ACE account, with an example for every step.

From creating your account to booking a delivery and reading your stock. The examples follow one made-up company, Acme Home Sp. z o.o., so you can see each step with real numbers. Stuck on something? Write to hello@2ace.pl or call +48 608 180 946.

The customerAcme Home Sp. z o.o., a Warsaw company selling ceramic mugs. Owner: Anna Kowalska.
Their productsMUG-BLUE "Blue ceramic mug" and MUG-RED "Red ceramic mug".

On this page

1. Create your account

Everything starts at 2ace.pl/login. You create one login for your company, then build a plan and pay.

  1. Open Log in and choose the Create account tab.
  2. Fill in your details: full name, email, phone, company name, country, tax or VAT ID, address, postal code and city. If your country is Poland, you also pick your voivodeship.
  3. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and click Create account.
  4. Open the email we send and click the confirmation link. It brings you back to the login page.
  5. Log in with your email and password.
Example

Anna fills in: Anna Kowalska, anna@acmehome.pl, +48 600 100 200, company Acme Home Sp. z o.o., country Poland, NIP 5252000000, voivodeship Mazowieckie, ul. Prosta 1, 00-001 Warszawa. After clicking the link in her email she logs in and lands in her dashboard.

If you forget your password

On the login page type your email, then click Forgot password?. We email you a link to choose a new one. You can also change your password any time under Account.

Your account page

Open 2ace.pl/account from the menu. You can edit your personal details, your company details, upload a company logo (it appears as your avatar), see invoices, open billing, and change your password.

Example

Anna uploads acme-logo.png under Company logo. From then on, her dashboard header shows the logo and "Welcome, Acme Home Sp. z o.o.".

2. Build your plan

You choose what you need in four steps and see the monthly price update as you go. Nothing is charged until you sign and pay.

Step 1: Space

Space is what you store goods in. You choose how many square metres you need, from 1 to 1,000 m². Storage costs 300 zł per m² a month. You type the number or drag the slider, and the picture fills one square for every m².

Example

Anna sells mugs and expects about 6,000 small items. She types 6,000 into "Not sure? Estimate from your stock", picks "Shoebox", and the builder suggests 18 m². She clicks Use 18 m². Storage shows 5 400 zł a month. A month later she needs less, so she changes the plan to 12 m² and pays 3 600 zł.

Step 2: Services

  • Fulfilment and returns are pay as you go. There is nothing to choose and no monthly fee. We pick, pack and hand over every order and charge a handling fee per order by the size of the packed parcel (a small parcel costs less than a big one, like on the marketplaces). Each return we receive costs a handling fee per return, also by parcel size. Shipping labels are the carrier's price plus a small fee. It all goes on your monthly invoice. The current prices are shown in the builder.
  • Import and customs: sea, air and road freight with customs cleared into Poland. It is quoted for each shipment, so it adds nothing to the monthly price.

Step 3: Sell

  • .pl storefront: we build and host your online shop. 199 zł per month plus a 2,950 zł one-time setup. The setup includes your .pl domain, so you choose a name here and we check it is free.

Step 4: Review, sign and pay

  1. Check the lines and the totals. All prices are net, without VAT.
  2. Sign in or create your account if you have not yet. Your plan is saved while you do.
  3. Read the service agreement summary, tick the box and type your full name to sign. If your plan has a storefront, signing also accepts Hostinger's domain registration agreement, because your .pl domain is registered through Hostinger in your company's name.
  4. Click Continue to secure payment. You pay on Stripe's page with a card, Apple Pay, Przelewy24 or SEPA.
  5. When the payment clears, your plan goes live. This usually takes a few seconds.
Example: Acme's plan

Acme chooses 14.4 m² and a storefront. Fulfilment and returns cost nothing a month: they are charged per order and per return.

Good to know

The plan is billed monthly with no minimum term. You can change or cancel with 30 days notice. Your draft plan is remembered in your browser, so you can leave and come back.

4. Add and manage products

Every product you want us to store needs an entry before you can book a delivery of it.

Add a product

  1. Open Products and click Add a product.
  2. Enter a SKU (your own code, up to 60 characters) and a name. The barcode (EAN) is optional but speeds up scanning at our warehouse.
  3. Add up to 4 photos. The first one becomes the thumbnail in your lists.
  4. Click Save product. It shows at once, with 0 in stock.
Example

SKU MUG-BLUE, name Blue ceramic mug, barcode 5901234123457, two photos. Then the same for MUG-RED. Each SKU can be used once in your account. Another company can use the same SKU for its own product.

Change or remove a product

New products appear immediately. Changes to products that already exist are requests that our team approves, so your catalogue stays reliable for the warehouse.

  1. On the product's row click Request edit.
  2. Change the name, add another barcode, or choose Request switch off or Request delete.
  3. Click Send for approval. The row now says "Waiting for approval". You can click Cancel request any time before we decide.
  4. We approve or decline. You get an email either way. If declined, the reason shows on the row.
Example

Anna renames MUG-BLUE to "Blue ceramic mug 350 ml". The row shows "Waiting for approval: Edit product MUG-BLUE". An hour later Piotr's colleague approves it and Anna gets an email "Approved: Edit product MUG-BLUE". The new name shows in her list.

Rules

A product that was ever on a delivery or in stock cannot be deleted. Ask us to switch it off instead. It stays in your history and no longer appears when you book deliveries.

Photos are the exception: you add or remove product photos yourself, with no approval, because they only describe the product. In the edit panel use Add photos or Remove. A product holds at most 4 photos.

5. Book and follow a delivery

An inbound delivery is goods on their way to our warehouse. Book it before it arrives so the team knows what to expect.

Book a delivery

  1. Open Inbound and click Book a delivery. Your plan must be active and you need at least one product.
  2. Set the expected arrival date, the carrier and a tracking number if you have one.
  3. Under "What is arriving" choose a product, enter the units and click Add. Repeat for each product. Each product can appear once per delivery.
  4. Click Book delivery.
Example

Expected 10 Nov 2026, carrier DSV, tracking DSV-4821937. Lines: MUG-BLUE × 600 and MUG-RED × 400. The delivery appears as IN-000012, status Booked, 1,000 units.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaning

Being receivedThe goods are at our dock and being counted.
ReceivedCounting is finished and the delivery is closed.
CancelledThe delivery was cancelled.

Change or delete a booked delivery

While a delivery is still Booked, click Request edit to change the date, carrier, tracking, products or units, or Request delete to remove it. Like product changes, these are sent for approval and the delivery stays as booked until we approve. Once goods have arrived, a delivery can no longer be changed.

Example

The supplier can only send 500 MUG-RED. Anna clicks Request edit on IN-000012, changes MUG-RED from 400 to 500, and clicks Send for approval. She sees "Waiting for approval: Edit delivery IN-000012".

When something is different on arrival

If fewer or more units arrive than you booked, goods arrive damaged, or an item was not on the booking, we record a difference. You see a red line on the delivery, and we email you.

Example

Marta counts 590 good MUG-BLUE and 10 cracked. Anna's delivery now shows: Differences found: MUG-BLUE: 590 of 600 arrived · MUG-BLUE: 10 damaged, with links Photo 1 and Photo 2 showing the damage. The photo links stay valid for an hour each time you open the page.

7. Place and follow orders

Orders are the parcels you want us to send to your buyers. We reserve the stock for each order the moment you place it.

If we ask you to correct an order

When the courier cannot accept an order's delivery details (for example the recipient's phone number, the postal code or the house number), we email you what to fix and show the same note on the order in your Orders tab. Reply to our email with the correct details. We update the order and create the label. Your goods stay reserved and nothing is charged for this.

Place one order

  1. Open Orders and click New order. Your plan must be active and your products must be on our shelves.
  2. Enter your own order number (optional but recommended), the recipient's first name and surname (letters only, for example Jan Kowalski), street with the house number, postal code, city, a two-letter country code such as PL or DE and the recipient's phone number (9 digits for Poland). The order is refused until these are right, because the courier's label cannot be made without them. Email is optional. Spaces, +48 and a leading 0 in the phone are fine, we tidy them.
  3. Choose each product and quantity and click Add. The list shows how many of each you have available.
  4. Click Place order.
Example

Anna places order SHOP-1001 for Jan Nowak, Prosta 1, 00-001 Warszawa, PL: MUG-BLUE × 2 and MUG-RED × 1. We have both on the shelf, so the order shows Reserved, waiting to be picked straight away.

All of it or none of it

We reserve an order only when every line can be filled. If one line is short, nothing is reserved, the order goes On hold with the reason, and we email you. It is reserved automatically when more stock arrives on our shelves, oldest orders first. Stock that is still in our receiving area, or in quarantine, cannot be sold yet.

Example

Anna orders MUG-BLUE × 7 and MUG-RED × 1, but only 6 blue mugs are on the shelf. The order is on hold: MUG-BLUE: need 7, available 6. The red mug is not reserved either. When her next delivery is received, the order is reserved by itself.

Import many orders from a CSV file

Every order needs what the courier's label needs. The file must have these columns: order_ref, name (first name and surname), phone (9 digits for Poland), address (street with the house number), postal, city, country, sku and qty. An order with a missing or wrong detail is refused with the reason, and the other orders in the file still import.

  1. Click Import CSV and Download the template.
  2. Fill one row per product. Rows with the same order_ref become one order. Match products by sku. Excel files saved with semicolons work too.
  3. Choose the file. You see how many orders are ready. Click Import orders.
  4. Every order gets its own result line: reserved, on hold, already imported, or the exact reason it failed. The good ones are kept even if others fail.
Example

A file with SHOP-1001 (two rows) and SHOP-1002 imports as two orders. Importing the same file again creates nothing new: both come back as "Already imported".

Cancel an order

Click Request cancellation. Like other changes, it needs our approval, and you get an email with the result. Once picking has started an order can no longer be cancelled here.

8. Ship your orders

Every order leaves with a shipping label. There are two ways to get it: you buy it yourself in your dashboard, or you give us your own.

If a label cannot be created because of the delivery details (for example a phone number that is too short), we email you what to correct and show the same note on the order. Reply with the correct details and we fix the order. Nothing is charged for a failed attempt.

What an order costs

Two things, both excluding VAT, both on your monthly invoice: the handling fee (packing, one per order, by the size of the packed parcel; each extra parcel in the same order adds a small amount) and the shipping label (carrier price plus a small fee). The handling fee is charged when the order ships. You can see every charge, with its status, under Returns in Usage charges on your account.

Example

In October Acme ships 900 small orders (class XS, 3,20 zł) and 40 larger ones (class L, 8,50 zł): 2 880 + 340 = 3 220 zł of handling fees, plus the labels it bought, all on the monthly invoice.

Buy the label yourself

You choose the carrier. The price is the carrier's price plus our small fee, shown before you buy, plus VAT, and it is added to your monthly invoice.

  1. Open Orders and click Shipping under a reserved order.
  2. Under Ship with 2ACE check the parcel: weight in kilograms and length, width and height in centimetres. It starts from the sizes you entered for your products. Weigh and measure the packed parcel if you can.
  3. Click Get prices. You see each carrier that can take the parcel (InPost, DPD, DHL, GLS and others) with its price before VAT.
  4. Click Buy on the one you want and confirm the price. If the price changed meanwhile, you are told and nothing is bought.
  5. The panel now shows the label with its tracking number. Click Download the label if you want a copy. We print it and stick it on the parcel when we pack.
Example

Anna's order ORD-000031 is two blue mugs. The panel suggests 1,75 kg and 12 × 10 × 36 cm from her product sizes. She gets prices: DPD 13,00 zł + VAT, InPost courier 14,50 zł + VAT. She buys DPD, and the label with tracking WB123 appears on the order. At the end of the month her invoice includes 13,00 zł + VAT for it.

Good to know

When we pack the order we weigh and measure the real parcel. If it is clearly bigger or heavier than you entered, the carrier may charge extra: we add that to your invoice, with the reason. Declare the real parcel to avoid it. A label bought here cannot be cancelled in the dashboard: write to us. You cannot cancel an order that already has a bought label. Only owner, operations and staff members can buy. For safety we limit the number of labels per day and the total not yet invoiced; if you reach a limit, the screen says so. If buying is not open for your account yet, the panel offers only your own label.

Or use your own label

If you already have a label for an order (from Allegro, InPost, Amazon or your own carrier contract), give it to us. We print it, put it on the parcel and hand it to the courier. We do not charge for shipping when you bring the label.

  1. Open Orders and click Shipping under the order. It is available while the order is received, reserved, being picked or packed.
  2. Click Choose file and select the label as a PDF of at most 2 MB.
  3. Optionally add the carrier name and the tracking number(s), separated by commas. You can add tracking numbers without a file too.
  4. Click Save the label. You see "Saved" and the label on file. The button on the order now reads Shipping (own label added).
Example

Anna sells on Allegro. For ORD-000014 she downloads the InPost label PDF from Allegro, opens Shipping on that order, chooses the file, types "InPost" and the tracking number, and saves. When Marta packs the order she prints Anna's label and sticks it on the box. Marta then marks the order shipped.

Changing or removing the label

Upload another file and click Replace the label, or click Remove. This works until the order is shipped. After that the order cannot get a label any more.

Good to know

Only owner, operations and staff members of your company can add labels. The file must be a real PDF. If your plan does not include fulfilment, the Shipping panel says so and we do not ship your orders. A label from you replaces ours for that order only; we never buy a second label for an order that has yours.

9. Returns

When your buyer sends something back, you announce it here and issue the return label yourself. We receive the parcel, inspect it, put good goods back on your shelf and tell you what happened.

Announce a return and issue the label

  1. Open Returns and click New return.
  2. Choose the shipped order the goods came from. The buyer's name, phone and address are filled in from the order: check them, because the carrier collects from this address. Enter how many of each product are coming back and, if you like, the reason. Click Announce the return.
  3. On the new return click Issue the return label, enter the weight and size of the parcel, click Get prices and Buy the carrier you want after confirming the price. The label is made for a carrier pickup from your buyer's address to our warehouse.
  4. Click Download the label and send it to your buyer.

What happens next

  • When the parcel arrives we weigh it and check every line. A line that is fine goes back on your shelf (grade A) and is available again. A line that is damaged or not sellable (grade B or C) is set aside in quarantine, with a note, and we ask you what to do with it.
  • You see the result on the return and receive an email. We aim to finish within 48 hours of arrival.
  • We charge one handling fee per return, by the size of the parcel that arrived, on your next invoice.
  • You can cancel a return until its label is bought. A return label you bought cannot be cancelled in the dashboard: write to us.
Example

Anna's buyer wants to return 2 blue mugs from ORD-000014. Anna announces RET-000003, issues a DPD return label for 13,00 zł + VAT and sends it to the buyer. A week later the parcel arrives: one mug is fine and goes back on her shelf, one is cracked and is set aside. Anna sees "1 back on your shelf, 1 set aside (damaged)" and a handling fee of 6,30 zł + VAT for the return on her next invoice.

Good to know

Only owner, operations and staff members can announce returns and buy labels. The buyer's name must be a first name and surname and the phone a real number, or the carrier refuses the label. If return labels are not open for your account yet, the panel says so and you can send the goods back yourself.

10. Change your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade yourself. We show the difference first and you confirm once.

  1. Click Build plan in the top bar. A banner says "You already have an active plan". Click Change plan. The same button is on the Overview and the Storefront tab. The builder opens with your current plan.
  2. Change what you need and go to the Review step. A badge shows Upgrade, Downgrade or No price change.
  3. Read the sentence about what is charged today and your new monthly price. Click Confirm.

Three examples

Upgrade

Acme has 14.4 m² at 4,320 zł and adds fulfillment: the monthly price becomes 9,360 zł, a difference of 5,040 zł. With half of the billing month left, the review says: Charged today: about 2,520 zł. From your next invoice you pay 9,360 zł a month. The card on file is charged straight away, and the invoice is emailed.

Downgrade

Acme goes from 14.4 to 7.2 m²: 4,320 zł becomes 2,160 zł. With half the month left, the review says: You get a credit of about 1,080 zł on your next invoice. Nothing is charged today.

No price change

Acme adds Import and customs. It has no monthly price (it is quoted per shipment), so the badge says "No price change" and the review says "Nothing is charged today". Your plan is updated and your monthly price stays the same.

Good to know

Adding a storefront needs a .pl domain name and adds the one-time 2,950 zł setup. If the card payment fails, the plan is not changed and you are not charged.

11. Get a .pl domain

If your active plan has no domain yet, a New badge appears on the Domain tab.

  1. Open Domain. Type the name you want, without .pl, and click Check. We check it against the Polish registry.
  2. If it is taken, suggested alternatives appear. Click one to check it.
  3. Tick the box to accept Hostinger's Domain Name Registration Agreement. We register your domain through Hostinger, in your company's name.
  4. If your plan has a storefront the domain is included: click Request my included domain. Otherwise click Pay 99 zł and request. The 99 zł is one time, net, and covers the first year.
  5. We register the domain and email you within one working day. The tab then shows the domain and its status.
Example

Anna types acmehome. The answer: acmehome.pl is already registered. Try: and two suggestions. She clicks acmehome-sklep.pl, which looks available, ticks the box, pays 99 zł on Stripe, and sees "Payment received. We are registering your domain".
